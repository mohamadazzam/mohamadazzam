## About

Aerospace Engineer with a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering (100% English-medium instruction, Türkiye) and 5+ completed internships across the aerospace sector. My work centers on developing and validating numerical solvers for multi-fluid, high-speed, and plasma-coupled flows — combining formal CFD methods with a hands-on electrical/electronics background for experimental plasma diagnostics.

**Currently seeking:** a funded M.S. / Graduate Research Assistantship (Fall 2027) in computational aerothermodynamics, multi-fluid CFD, or aerospace magnetohydrodynamics.

---

## Research Focus

| | |
|---|---|
| **Computational Fluid Dynamics** | Multi-fluid Euler/Navier–Stokes solver development, Riemann solvers (Roe, HLLC, HLLD), high-order schemes (TVD, WENO) |
| **Aerothermodynamics & MHD** | Hypersonic/high-speed flows, real-gas and thermochemical non-equilibrium effects, flow control, aerospace plasma interactions |
| **Verification & Validation** | Grid Convergence Index (GCI) studies, analytical benchmarking, validation against experimental/published reference data |
| **Experimental & Hardware** | Discharge device design, circuit wiring, microcontroller-based (Arduino) DAQ for plasma diagnostics |
